01 The operating problem

The work happens in the field. The records stay scattered.

Many local service teams still coordinate through notebooks, phone calls and disconnected messages. Customer history gets lost, preventive service slips, and owners cannot easily verify what actually happened at a visit.

01

Missed follow-ups

AMC dates and service history are hard to track reliably across a growing customer list.

02

Limited field visibility

Job status depends on calls and manual updates rather than a shared record.

03

Incomplete proof of work

Parts, readings, photos, signatures and invoices end up in separate places.

02 The product

One workflow for the shop owner and the field technician.

Paani Boss connects day-to-day service operations without pretending to replace the people doing the work.

Owner dashboard

Manage customers, assign jobs, follow service activity and keep operational records together.

Technician jobs

Give field staff a clear list of assigned visits with job details and navigation.

Visit verification

Apply a 200-metre location check before a technician closes an on-site job.

Service scheduling

Use TDS readings, water source and service history to support the next-service date.

Proof and billing

Record parts used, readings, before-and-after photos, customer signatures and invoices.

Route planning

Organise the day's assigned visits into a more practical technician route.
